# Northwind Global — Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

**Document ID:** NW-POL-TRV-004
**Version:** 4.2
**Effective date:** 1 April 2026
**Supersedes:** v4.1 (1 April 2025)
**Policy owner:** Global Finance — Travel & Expense (travel@northwind.example)
**Applies to:** All employees, contingent workers and interns of Northwind Global and its
wholly-owned subsidiaries, worldwide.

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy defines how Northwind Global employees book, undertake and claim reimbursement for
business travel. It exists to keep travellers safe, to keep spend predictable, and to ensure the
company meets its tax and audit obligations in every jurisdiction in which it operates.

### 1.2 Who this policy covers

This policy applies to all employees, contingent workers and interns travelling on Northwind
business. It applies regardless of who ultimately bears the cost, including travel that is
rebilled to a client.

### 1.3 What this policy does not cover

The following are governed by separate documents and are out of scope here:

- Relocation and permanent transfer costs — see *NW-POL-HR-011 Relocation*.
- Immigration, visa sponsorship and work permits — see *NW-POL-HR-019 Global Mobility*.
- Client entertainment and gifts — see *NW-POL-FIN-007 Anti-Bribery and Entertainment*.
- Personal vehicle insurance arrangements — see *NW-POL-FIN-022 Fleet*.
- Conference sponsorship and exhibition budgets — these are marketing spend, not travel.

### 1.4 Employee grades referenced in this policy

| Grade band | Titles included |
|---|---|
| Band 1 | Intern, Associate, Analyst |
| Band 2 | Senior Analyst, Engineer, Consultant |
| Band 3 | Senior Engineer, Manager, Senior Consultant |
| Band 4 | Director, Senior Manager, Principal |
| Band 5 | Vice President, Senior Director |
| Band 6 | Executive Committee, C-suite |

Throughout this policy, "Director level and above" means **Band 4, 5 or 6**.

## 2. General Principles

### 2.1 The reasonable-person test

Travellers must spend the company's money as carefully as they would spend their own. Where this
policy is silent, apply the reasonable-person test and seek written approval from your line
manager before committing spend.

### 2.2 Booking through the approved channel

All flights, rail and hotels **must** be booked through the Northwind Travel Portal (powered by
our travel management company, Gateway Travel). Bookings made outside the portal are
**not reimbursable** except in the circumstances listed in section 2.3.

### 2.3 Permitted out-of-portal bookings

Out-of-portal booking is permitted, and reimbursable, only where:

a) the Travel Portal is unavailable for more than four consecutive hours and the travel is due to
   commence within 48 hours;
b) the traveller is already in transit and must rebook due to a disruption (see section 9.2);
c) a client contractually requires the use of their own nominated booking agency; or
d) the total trip cost is under USD 150 and the supplier does not appear in the portal.

In every case the traveller must attach a written explanation to the expense claim.

### 2.4 Advance booking windows

Travel must be booked at the following minimum notice. Bookings made inside the window require
line-manager approval recorded in the portal.

| Travel type | Minimum advance booking |
|---|---|
| Domestic flights | 14 days |
| International flights | 21 days |
| Rail | 7 days |
| Hotel only | 3 days |

Bookings made inside these windows without approval may be reduced to the fare that *would* have
applied at the correct notice, with the difference borne by the traveller's cost centre.

## 3. Approvals

### 3.1 Standard approval chain

| Estimated total trip cost (USD) | Approver required |
|---|---|
| Up to 2,500 | Line manager |
| 2,501 – 10,000 | Line manager **and** Department Head |
| 10,001 – 25,000 | Department Head **and** Finance Business Partner |
| Above 25,000 | Executive Committee sponsor |

Trip cost means the total of flights, rail, hotel, ground transport and estimated per diems for
the whole trip, for one traveller.

### 3.2 Multi-traveller approval

Where **five or more** employees travel to the same destination for the same event, the trip
requires Department Head approval regardless of individual cost, and a single consolidated
booking request must be raised.

### 3.3 Approval validity

An approval lapses if travel has not commenced within 90 days of the approval date, or if the
estimated trip cost rises by more than 20% from the approved figure. In either case a fresh
approval is required.

## 4. Air Travel

### 4.1 Fare selection

Travellers must select the **lowest logical fare** on the preferred routing. A fare is "logical"
if it does not add more than two hours to total journey time, does not add an additional
connection, and does not require departure before 06:00 or arrival after 23:00 local time.

### 4.2 Cabin class entitlement

Cabin class is determined by **both** scheduled flight duration **and** employee grade. Scheduled
flight duration means the total scheduled time in the air for the longest single leg of the
journey, excluding layovers.

| Longest single leg | Band 1–2 | Band 3 | Band 4–5 | Band 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 4 hours | Economy | Economy | Economy | Business |
| 4 to 6 hours | Economy | Economy | Premium Economy | Business |
| Over 6 hours and up to 10 hours | Premium Economy | Premium Economy | Business | Business |
| Over 10 hours | Premium Economy | Business | Business | Business |

First Class is not permitted for any grade, including Band 6, without written Executive Committee
approval obtained in advance.

### 4.3 Red-eye provision

Where a flight departs between 22:00 and 05:00 local time and has a scheduled duration over six
hours, travellers of **any** grade may book one cabin class above their standard entitlement in
section 4.2, capped at Business. This provision may be used at most **four times per calendar
year** per traveller.

### 4.4 Seat selection and ancillaries

- Standard seat selection is reimbursable on all flights.
- Extra-legroom or exit-row seats are reimbursable only on flights with a scheduled duration over
  four hours, and only in Economy or Premium Economy.
- One checked bag per traveller is reimbursable. A second checked bag is reimbursable only for
  trips of **10 nights or longer**, or where the traveller is carrying company equipment.
- Lounge access is reimbursable only for Band 5 and above, or during a delay exceeding three hours.
- In-flight Wi-Fi is reimbursable on flights over four hours.

### 4.5 Airline loyalty programmes

Travellers keep personal loyalty points earned on business travel. Points must never influence
carrier selection where this results in a higher fare. Upgrades purchased with personal points are
permitted at no cost to the company and do not require approval.

### 4.6 Carbon and routing

Where two fares are within 10% of each other, travellers should select the itinerary with the
lower carbon estimate as displayed in the Travel Portal. Direct flights are preferred over
connecting itineraries where the fare difference does not exceed USD 200.

## 5. Rail Travel

### 5.1 When rail is required instead of air

For journeys where a rail option exists with a scheduled city-centre-to-city-centre time of
**under four hours**, rail must be booked in preference to flying, unless the total rail fare
exceeds the lowest logical air fare by more than 40%.

### 5.2 Rail class entitlement

| Journey duration | Band 1–3 | Band 4 and above |
|---|---|---|
| Under 3 hours | Standard | Standard |
| 3 hours or more | Standard | First Class |

Where First Class includes a meal that would otherwise be claimed as a per diem meal, the
corresponding per diem meal deduction in section 7.4 applies.

### 5.3 Rail cards and season tickets

Annual rail cards are reimbursable where the traveller can demonstrate that projected business
rail spend over 12 months exceeds the cost of the card. Season tickets for commuting are **not**
reimbursable — commuting is not business travel (see section 12.1).

## 6. Accommodation

### 6.1 Nightly rate caps

Caps are inclusive of room rate and mandatory taxes and resort fees, exclusive of meals. Caps are
per room per night in USD or local equivalent at the portal's exchange rate on the booking date.

| City tier | Examples | Nightly cap (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Singapore, Zurich, Hong Kong | 350 |
| Tier 2 | Chicago, Boston, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, Sydney, Dubai, Toronto | 275 |
| Tier 3 | Austin, Manchester, Lisbon, Barcelona, Kuala Lumpur, Warsaw, Bengaluru | 190 |
| Tier 4 | All other locations | 150 |

### 6.2 Exceeding the cap

The nightly cap may be exceeded by up to 25% without additional approval where the traveller is
staying at the venue of a conference or client site and cheaper compliant options are more than
30 minutes' travel away. Beyond 25%, Department Head approval is required in advance.

### 6.3 Conference room blocks

Where a conference offers a negotiated room block, travellers may book it at the block rate even
if that rate exceeds the section 6.1 cap, provided the block rate is documented in the claim. This
is an explicit exception to section 6.1.

### 6.4 Room type and extras

- Standard rooms only. Suites and club floors are not reimbursable below Band 5.
- In-room internet is reimbursable.
- Mini-bar, in-room movies, spa and gym day-passes are **not** reimbursable.
- Hotel laundry is reimbursable on trips of **five nights or longer**.
- Hotel breakfast is reimbursable where included in the room rate; where charged separately it
  must be claimed against the breakfast per diem in section 7.

### 6.5 Staying with friends or family

Travellers who stay privately instead of in a hotel may claim a flat **USD 45 per night** in lieu
of accommodation, with no receipt required. This may not be claimed for more than 14 nights in a
calendar year.

## 7. Meals and Per Diems

### 7.1 How per diems work

Northwind operates a **per diem** system for meals and incidentals. Travellers receive a fixed
daily allowance based on destination city tier. **Receipts are not required for per diem claims.**
Travellers may not claim actual meal costs in addition to a per diem for the same day.

### 7.2 Daily per diem rates

| City tier | Breakfast | Lunch | Dinner | Incidentals | Full day total (USD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 20 | 28 | 47 | 15 | 110 |
| Tier 2 | 17 | 23 | 38 | 12 | 90 |
| Tier 3 | 13 | 18 | 29 | 10 | 70 |
| Tier 4 | 10 | 14 | 22 | 9 | 55 |

City tiers are the same as those defined in section 6.1.

### 7.3 Partial days

On the day of departure and the day of return, travellers claim only the meals that fall within
their travel window:

- Departure before 07:00 — breakfast, lunch and dinner may be claimed.
- Departure between 07:00 and 13:00 — lunch and dinner may be claimed.
- Departure after 13:00 — dinner only.
- Return before 13:00 — breakfast only.
- Return between 13:00 and 19:00 — breakfast and lunch.
- Return after 19:00 — breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Incidentals are claimable in full on any day involving travel.

### 7.4 Provided meals

Where a meal is provided — by an airline in a premium cabin, included in a hotel rate, provided at
a conference, or hosted by a client — the corresponding per diem component **must be deducted**
from that day's claim. Deducting provided meals is the traveller's responsibility and is checked
on audit.

### 7.5 Team meals and hosting

A traveller hosting other Northwind employees may claim the actual cost of the meal instead of per
diems, up to **USD 75 per attendee** inclusive of service and tax, with an itemised receipt and
the names of all attendees. All attendees must then deduct the corresponding per diem component
for that meal. Alcohol within a hosted team meal is reimbursable up to two drinks per attendee.

### 7.6 Alcohol

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a hosted team meal under section 7.5 or a client meal
under the Entertainment policy. Alcohol purchased individually is not reimbursable and is not
covered by the per diem — the per diem is intended for food and non-alcoholic drinks.

## 8. Ground Transport

### 8.1 Order of preference

Travellers should use, in order of preference: public transport, then ride-hailing or taxi, then
rental car, then personal vehicle.

### 8.2 Ride-hailing and taxis

Standard service tiers only (e.g. UberX, Lyft Standard, Grab Car). Premium tiers are reimbursable
only where the traveller is carrying company equipment, travelling between 22:00 and 05:00, or
travelling with a client. Single journeys over **USD 120** require an explanatory note.

### 8.3 Rental cars

| Grade band | Maximum vehicle class |
|---|---|
| Band 1–3 | Intermediate / Compact |
| Band 4–5 | Standard / Full-size |
| Band 6 | Premium |

Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) and Third-Party Liability must always be accepted and are
reimbursable. Prepaid fuel options are not reimbursable — refuel before returning the vehicle.
Rental cars must not be used for journeys over 500 km in a single day; book rail or air instead.

### 8.4 Personal vehicle mileage

Where a personal vehicle is used for business travel, mileage is reimbursed at the published
Northwind rate for that country, reviewed annually each January.

| Country | Rate |
|---|---|
| United States | USD 0.67 per mile |
| United Kingdom | GBP 0.45 per mile (first 10,000 miles), GBP 0.25 thereafter |
| Eurozone | EUR 0.42 per kilometre |
| India | INR 22.00 per kilometre |

Mileage reimbursement covers fuel, wear and insurance. Fuel receipts must **not** be claimed
separately in addition to mileage.

### 8.5 Parking and tolls

Airport parking is reimbursable for trips up to **seven days**; beyond seven days, travellers
should use a taxi or ride-hail to the airport where that is cheaper than the parking cost. Tolls
and congestion charges incurred on business journeys are reimbursable.

## 9. Travel Disruption and Safety

### 9.1 Duty of care

Travellers must keep their itinerary in the Travel Portal current so that Northwind can locate
them in an emergency. The 24-hour emergency assistance line is staffed by Gateway Travel and is
printed on the reverse of the Northwind travel card.

### 9.2 Disruption rebooking

Where a flight or train is cancelled or delayed such that the traveller will miss a business
commitment, the traveller may rebook directly with the carrier without prior approval and claim
the cost. The rebooked service must not exceed the cabin class entitlement in section 4.2, except
where the entitled cabin is unavailable on any service arriving in time.

### 9.3 Overnight disruption

Where disruption forces an unplanned overnight stay, travellers may book accommodation up to the
section 6.1 cap for the location without prior approval, and claim a full day per diem for the
additional day. Where the carrier provides accommodation, it must be used.

### 9.4 High-risk destinations

Travel to destinations rated **High** or **Extreme** on the Gateway Travel risk index requires
Security Team clearance in addition to the normal approval chain in section 3.1, obtained no later
than 10 working days before departure. The list of rated destinations is maintained in the Travel
Portal and is not reproduced in this policy.

### 9.5 Travel insurance

All employees travelling on Northwind business are covered by the corporate travel insurance
policy. Personal travel insurance purchased separately is **not** reimbursable.

## 10. Combining Business and Personal Travel

### 10.1 Permitted

Travellers may extend a business trip for personal reasons provided there is **no incremental cost
to Northwind**. The traveller must obtain a portal quotation for the business-only itinerary; the
company reimburses the lower of the actual fare and the business-only fare.

### 10.2 Accommodation and per diems on personal days

Accommodation, per diems and ground transport are **not** claimable for personal days. Where a
Saturday-night stay demonstrably reduces the total airfare by more than the cost of the additional
night's accommodation and per diem, that additional night **is** claimable — attach the fare
comparison.

### 10.3 Travelling companions

Costs for spouses, partners, family members or other companions are never reimbursable. Where a
companion's presence increases a room rate, the traveller bears the difference.

## 11. Expense Claims and Reimbursement

### 11.1 Submission deadline

Expense claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the trip end date. Claims
submitted between 31 and 60 days require Department Head approval with a written explanation.
Claims submitted **more than 60 days** after the trip end date will not be reimbursed except in
documented cases of extended illness or leave.

### 11.2 Receipt requirements

| Expense type | Receipt required |
|---|---|
| Per diem meals | No |
| Flights, rail, hotel | Always (itemised) |
| Ground transport | Above USD 25 |
| Any other expense | Above USD 25 |

An itemised receipt shows the vendor, date, individual line items and total. A credit-card slip
showing only a total is **not** an itemised receipt and is not acceptable for hotel or hosted-meal
claims.

### 11.3 Currency and exchange rates

Claims are submitted in the currency incurred. The expense system converts using the corporate
card's settlement rate where the corporate card was used, or the European Central Bank reference
rate on the transaction date otherwise. Travellers must not apply their own exchange rates.

### 11.4 Reimbursement timing

Approved claims are paid in the next scheduled payment run. Payment runs occur on the **15th and
the last working day** of each month. Claims approved after the 12th or after the third-to-last
working day may fall into the following run.

### 11.5 Corporate cards

Employees expected to travel more than twice a year are issued a corporate card. Corporate cards
must be used for all reimbursable travel spend where accepted. Personal use of the corporate card
is prohibited and repeated misuse may result in withdrawal of the card.

## 12. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

### 12.1 Never reimbursable

The following are never reimbursable under this policy:

- Commuting between home and the traveller's normal place of work.
- Traffic fines, parking fines, speeding penalties and towing charges.
- Costs for travelling companions (section 10.3).
- Personal travel insurance (section 9.5).
- Mini-bar, in-room movies, spa treatments (section 6.4).
- Airline or hotel loyalty-programme membership fees.
- Lost or stolen personal property.
- Childcare, pet care and house-sitting costs incurred while travelling.
- Upgrades purchased with cash beyond the entitlement in section 4.2.
- Tips exceeding 20% of the pre-tax bill.
- Personal grooming, haircuts and clothing, except as provided in section 12.2.

### 12.2 Emergency clothing

Where checked baggage is delayed by more than 24 hours, travellers may claim up to **USD 200** for
essential clothing and toiletries, with itemised receipts. Any amount later recovered from the
carrier or insurer must be repaid to Northwind.

## 13. Exceptions and Policy Conflicts

### 13.1 Requesting an exception

Exceptions to any provision of this policy require **written** approval from both the traveller's
Department Head and the Finance Business Partner, obtained **in advance** of the spend. Retroactive
exception requests are considered only where the spend arose from circumstances outside the
traveller's control.

### 13.2 Order of precedence

Where provisions conflict, the following order of precedence applies, highest first:

1. Local statutory requirements in the jurisdiction of travel.
2. A written exception approved under section 13.1.
3. Specific provisions of this policy (sections 4 to 12).
4. The general principles in section 2.

Accordingly, a written exception approved under 13.1 **overrides** any cap or entitlement in
sections 4 to 12, but never overrides local statutory requirements.

### 13.3 Accessibility and medical accommodations

Travellers with a documented medical condition or disability may be granted standing exceptions,
including cabin class above entitlement, additional baggage or specific accommodation
requirements. These are arranged confidentially through HR and do not require per-trip approval.
Section 13.1's advance-approval requirement does not apply to accommodations arranged this way.

### 13.4 Breach of policy

Deliberate or repeated breach of this policy may result in non-reimbursement, withdrawal of the
corporate card and, in serious cases, disciplinary action under the Northwind Disciplinary
Procedure.

## 14. Governance

### 14.1 Review cycle

This policy is reviewed annually by Global Finance — Travel & Expense. Per diem rates and mileage
rates are reviewed each January and may be updated without a full policy revision; the rate tables
in sections 7.2 and 8.4 are authoritative as at the effective date on the cover.

### 14.2 Questions

Policy interpretation questions should be directed to travel@northwind.example. Booking and
itinerary questions should go to Gateway Travel through the Travel Portal.
